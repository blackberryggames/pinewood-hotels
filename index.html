NIGHT DESK
Hollow Pines Hotel · Gate Checkpoint · Night Shift

How to play

You work the night gate at the Hollow Pines Hotel. People walk up to your window all night. Not all of them are people.

  • Request ID and compare name, room and ID number against the Ledger. A resident already marked IN cannot be outside.
  • Guests must be on tonight's Arrivals list with a matching booking code. Nobody else gets in.
  • Look closely (hold right mouse). Eyes, teeth, necks, hands, breath, shadows.
  • Check the Camera. Some of them only show their real face on camera.
  • Use the Flashlight. Some of their eyes shine like an animal's.
  • Radio: R on/off, N next station, - and = volume.
  • Slam the Shutter (SPACE) if something turns on you. You have seconds.
  • 3 monsters inside and the hotel is lost. 3 violations and you're fired.

Controls: Mouse look · RMB/Z zoom · T talk · I ID · L ledger · C camera · 1/2 switch cam · F flashlight · SPACE shutter · A admit · D deny · ESC pause
